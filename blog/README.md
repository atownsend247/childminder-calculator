# Building a childcare hours calculator

A series on building the [Childcare Hours Calculator](../README.md), a single-page tool
that works out monthly childcare cost across one to four childminders, after funded
hours and bank holidays. Each post covers one real step in building it, in order.

1. [Why a static page, and why not a spreadsheet](01-why-a-static-page.md)
2. [From two childminders to four](02-from-two-childminders-to-four.md)
3. [Splitting free hours across childminders](03-splitting-free-hours.md)
4. [Spreading hours across the week, and the partial-week problem](04-weeks-and-partial-weeks.md)
5. [Term dates, persistence, and small decisions](05-term-dates-and-persistence.md)

The posts cover the work from 29 August to 2 September 2026. Each post ends with a hidden
HTML comment listing the commits it covers, so the series can be checked against
`git log`; blog-sync strips those before posting. The finance-system posts carry an
Unsplash banner comment under the title; these don't yet, so add one before syncing.
